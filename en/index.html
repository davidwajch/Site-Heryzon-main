WHAT IT DOES

Clear about what it does. And what it doesn't.

  • Handles on the call

    • Common questions (hours, locations, documents, procedures)
    • Bookings
    • Rescheduling
    • Cancellations
    • Call triage

  • Hands off to a person

    • Serious complaints
    • Negotiations
    • Sensitive situations
    • Any request without an approved rule

  • Not yet

    • Outbound calls
    • Sales
    • WhatsApp support

CONTROL

Your company decides what the assistant says.

  • Approved rules

    Bruno only answers and acts on the rules and documents your company has approved. Without a rule, it doesn't improvise.

  • A person always within reach

    Anything outside the rules goes to your team, with a summary of the call.

  • Everything logged

    Every call produces a record of the reason, what was done and a summary.

  • Transparency

    The assistant introduces itself as a virtual assistant. Nobody is misled.
